# Legal Liability and CRA Compliance Notice

This document clarifies the distribution of legal and operational responsibilities under the European Union **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** for the Medulla project[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

---

## 1. Open Source Source Code (Community)

The Medulla source code is made publicly available under the **GNU General Public License v3 (GPLv3)**[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

* **CRA Exemption**: Pursuant to the CRA provisions regarding free and open-source software developed outside the course of a commercial activity, the raw source code hosted in this repository is provided "AS IS", without warranty of any kind[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
* **Contributor Liability**: Individual developers, volunteer contributors, and community maintainers shall not be held legally liable for vulnerabilities, flaws, or malfunctions in the source code[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

---

## 2. Official Products and Commercial Distributions

The status of **Operator / Manufacturer** under the Cyber Resilience Act is assumed exclusively by the publishing company **NATSU**[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

NATSU bears all legal CRA compliance obligations solely for[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span):
* **Official signed binary installers** (Windows / Linux / macOS) produced and distributed by the company[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Hosted SaaS offerings** and managed services[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
* Customers with an active commercial subscription or support contract[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

These official products benefit from Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) availability, mandatory vulnerability reporting to competent authorities (ANSSI / ENISA) within 24 hours, and formal security support commitments[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
